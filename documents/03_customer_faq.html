How do I report suspected fraud?

Report suspected fraud as soon as you notice it, within 13 months of the transaction date, to remain eligible for a full investigation. You can report via the mobile app's "Report a Transaction" flow, by phone, or in branch.

What if I need to report fraud outside normal business hours?

For urgent fraud reports outside business hours (Mon-Fri 9am-6pm), call our 24/7 fraud team on 0345 300 0116. If you are contacted directly by a member of our fraud investigations team, you can reach the lead investigator, Priya Anand, at priya.anand@lloydsfinancial-training.example or on 07700 900123 to verify their identity before sharing any information.

How much am I liable for if my card is used without authorisation?

If a card is lost, stolen, or used without authorisation, your maximum liability before you notify the bank is £50, unless gross negligence is established. Once you report the loss, you have no further liability for transactions made after the report time.

What is authorised push payment (APP) fraud?

APP fraud is when a customer is deceived into authorising a payment themselves. Claims are assessed under the APP fraud reimbursement scheme. A standard claim is assessed within 5 working days; complex cases may take up to 35 working days, with the customer informed of any delay.

What if I disagree with a fraud investigation outcome?

You may request a review, and subsequently escalate to the Financial Ombudsman Service if the matter remains unresolved after 8 weeks.
